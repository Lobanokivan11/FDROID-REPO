Based on https://github.com/Axixi2233/moonlight-android

1. Translated Axixi's additions into English
2. Added option to disable gamepad battery report to potentially fix input stuttering issue
3. Added option to force apply refresh rate (fix locked 60fps on some devices)
4. Added natural track pad mode for touch screen
5. Fixed mouse mode disabling
6. Enhanced portrait mode
7. Improved physical keyboard usability:
	- Tap as click
	- Double tap as double click
	- Double tap to drag
	- Two finger tap as right click
	- Two finger scrolling
	- Two finger double tap as right click drag
	- Non-QWERTY keyboard layout support
	- Quick Meta key with physicl BACK button
